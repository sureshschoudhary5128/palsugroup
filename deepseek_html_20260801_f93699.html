FAQ

What is PALSU GROUP?
PALSU GROUP is the official technology community powered by PALSU TECHNOLOGY. It brings together developers, students, researchers, and innovators to collaborate on AI, software, web, automation, cybersecurity, and open-source projects.
What is PALSU TECHNOLOGY?
PALSU TECHNOLOGY is the innovation engine behind PALSU GROUP. It focuses on Artificial Intelligence, software development, web technologies, automation, cybersecurity, and research.
How can I join the team?
We welcome developers, researchers, designers, and technology enthusiasts. Reach out via email at community@palsugroup.com or connect with us on social platforms.
How can I contribute?
You can contribute by submitting pull requests, reporting issues, writing documentation, sharing ideas, or participating in community discussions. Every contribution is valued.
Is everything open source?
Yes! All core technologies and projects are open source and available on our GitHub organization. We believe in transparency and community-driven development.
